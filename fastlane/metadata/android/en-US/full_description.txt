ZeroGallery is an extremely lightweight, ad-free and 100% offline gallery app for your photos and videos.

No tracking. No internet access. No cloud. Just your media, your control.

FEATURES
• Fast, native scanning of photos & videos via Android's own MediaStore
• Smooth, adaptive grid with cached thumbnails - on phones and tablets alike
• Fullscreen viewer with swipe navigation, pinch- and double-tap-to-zoom
• Smooth video playback, with hidden-chrome gestures for seek, brightness and volume
• Group your gallery by date or by folder, with a fast-scroll thumb for large libraries
• Optionally read one or more hidden (dot-prefixed) folders too, via Android's own folder picker - no special permission needed
• Share or delete photos and videos directly from the grid or the fullscreen viewer

WHY NO INTERNET PERMISSION?
ZeroGallery deliberately does not declare the INTERNET permission. This isn't a setting you can toggle off later - it's an architectural guarantee: without this permission, the app is technically incapable of ever sending or receiving any data, no matter what the code does.
